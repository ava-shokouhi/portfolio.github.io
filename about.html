About Me & Objective

My name is Ava Shokouhi, I am currently a second year student at the University of Oregon studying Advertising and Computer Information and Technology. After college, I am pursuing my dream careers either as a fashion advertising strategist or as a web developer for fashion and beauty. I created this site as a place of inspiration and manifestation for my career goals and topics I am passionate about, such as textile waste, art, advertisements that energize me, and women's empowerment.

My area of interest is most definitely focused on the fashion and beauty industry. Specifically, project management and becoming an advertising strategist for sustainable and high end fashion companies. Also including web development for websites for high end fashion and beauty brands. I want to create advertisements for leading fashion and beauty brands that emphasize self expression, innovation, inclusivity, and sustainability. The way I feel when I wear clothes that accentuate who I am is one of the most liberating feelings I have ever felt. I want to make other women feel that way about the clothes they wear.
