Textile Waste

This image is originally from SIFO, which is a consumption research company based in Norway. SIFO is a company that studies the production of clothing, greenwashing, microplastic shedding, and clothing waste.

One of my career goals is to help spread more awareness to combat textile waste in fashion, especially within the fast fashion shopping community. In 2023, Earth.org reports that there is 1.92 million tonnes of textile waste produced each year. This means that these tonnes of materials and clothing get burned, dumped in our oceans, and put into landfills increasing gloabl emissons by 50% by 2030. The number of times an article of clothing is worn has decreased roughly by 36% in the last 15 years. In my own efforts to try and reduce clothing waste, I upcycle clothes and scrap fabrics into patchwork designs for myself and for my friends. I think fashion sustainability is extremely important as its killing our earth more rapidly than before.

Women Empowerment Through Sports and Fashion

Womens empowerment is something I am extremely passionate about. I recently became extremely inspired by the Nike commercial during the Super Bowl LIX, "So Win." I thought the message of this was exceedingly important in society and as a woman to continue to break boundaries in everything we do, including sports. I went on a deep dive of fashion advertisements that showcased women actively participating in sports whilst looking fashionable while doing so. Women can really do it all.
